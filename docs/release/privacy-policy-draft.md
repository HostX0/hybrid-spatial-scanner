# Hybrid Spatial Scanner — Privacy Policy

Draft for publication review. Effective date: to be confirmed at launch.

Hybrid Spatial Scanner helps you capture and review rooms and objects on your
device. You can use it without an account. The current application does not use
advertising, tracking or an analytics service, and does not send your scans to
an app-operated server.

## Camera and spatial information

When you choose to begin a scan, the app uses camera images and supported
device depth and motion information to reconstruct the space or object.
Projects may include images, geometry, dimensions, capture references and your
review notes. This information can reveal details about private places. Keep
your device secure and share scans only with people you trust.

## Local storage and deletion

Room projects and object captures are stored separately on your device. Object
captures retain source photographs so you can resume work or reconstruct a
model. Deleting a room does not delete a separate object capture.

Moving a project to Trash retains it until you choose permanent deletion.
Permanently deleting a project removes its local project data and related
exports. Deleting an object capture removes that session's local photographs,
checkpoint and model. These actions cannot erase copies you previously shared
or copies retained in device backups.

## Import, export and sharing

You choose which documents to import and which results to export or share.
Room archives include spatial observations, geometry and review notes. Object
model sharing does not include the retained source photographs. Portable room
archives remove the original device clock origin while preserving the scan's
event intervals and geometry.

Source images used for a room's color view remain local. Offline 3D exports
include the images used for that view only when you enable image inclusion;
it is off by default. Room archives currently preserve the measured scan
without those source color images. Permanent room deletion removes its local
source images along with the room's other data.

The app uses the system document picker and share sheet. Destinations you choose,
such as another app or cloud storage, handle their copies under their own
policies. Device backup settings may also determine where backup copies reside.

## Permissions and device operation

Camera permission is requested for scanning. You can manage it in device
settings. The app checks available storage and device pressure to protect your
work and pause capture when needed. These operational readings are not shared
as analytics or included in exported scans.

## Policy updates and contact

This policy must be updated before introducing an account, app-operated cloud
processing, telemetry or another data-sharing feature. A public support contact
and final effective date must be supplied before this draft is published.
