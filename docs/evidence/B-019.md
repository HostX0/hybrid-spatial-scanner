## Delivered portable area module

`hss::area::calculate` is a standalone C++20 consumer for one already-validated
counterclockwise outer ring and zero or more already-validated clockwise hole
rings. It does not depend on or modify the B-016 boundary module. It does not
close, intersect, reverse, reorder, simplify, hull, or invent topology.

The numeric path translates every ring to its first vertex and accumulates a
triangle-fan signed area using compensated long-double summation. Canonical
input coordinates are metres and the result explicitly reports square metres.
Every successful per-ring assessment retains the submitted ring identity,
signed contribution, source revision, support IDs, geometry origin, and quality
state.

The `internal_finished_face` policy reports the accepted outer area without
subtracting candidate holes. The `net_fixed_obstructions` policy subtracts a
hole only when it is classified as a fixed obstruction, has an explicit
`fixed` planning decision, and is detached. Attached and uncertain candidates,
furniture, other categories, and non-fixed candidates receive distinct
non-subtraction decisions. Unsupported classification enum values fail closed.

Incomplete topology returns a provisional result with null area. Rejected or
invalid topology returns an unavailable result with null area. All other
failures are atomic: no partial numeric area or ring assessments are exposed.
The calculator enforces bounded ring/vertex consumption, finite coordinates,
nonempty identities, and declared CCW/CW winding as safe-consumption checks; it
does not claim to replace B-016's containment or intersection validation.

## Synthetic fixture and test coverage

`fixtures/area/rib_with_fixed_column.csv` is explicitly synthetic. Its protected
rib outer contour has an exact mathematical area of 29.925 m2. Under the net
policy, the explicitly fixed detached 0.30 m by 0.50 m column is the only
subtraction, producing 29.775 m2.

The focused host test also covers the gross policy; attached, uncertain,
furniture, unclassified, and not-explicitly-fixed candidates; identity/unit/
provenance retention; incomplete and rejected topology; invalid enum values;
wrong winding; short and nonfinite rings; caller-declared resource limits; and
a 15 m2 outer ring plus 1 m2 hole translated to an origin of 1e12 metres. The
last case verifies the translated compensated calculation returns 14 m2 rather
than losing the local area to global-origin cancellation.

## Scope and limitations

This task adds no boundary-graph changes, C ABI/schema changes, engine facade,
Swift/Xcode changes, triangulation, automatic attachment classification,
physical capture, export integration, or production scene publication. The
caller remains responsible for proving that rings were accepted together by
the topology authority and that their semantic/fixed/attachment states belong
to the selected accepted scene revision.

simulator build, ARKit/RoomPlan session, real room scan, physical metrology,
accuracy/repeatability study, or thermal/performance test ran. Passing these
host sanitizer checks makes no physical measurement or production-scanner
claim.
