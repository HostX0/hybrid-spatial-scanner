# Hybrid Spatial Scanner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026.

## Who we are

Hybrid Spatial Scanner is provided by THE THIRD ACT LLC. Contact Hello@the-3rdact.com or +1 (719) 286-7476 for app support or privacy enquiries.

## On-device processing

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or an analytics service. Capture, reconstruction, project review and supported exports run on your device. The app does not automatically upload scans to a server operated by us.

## Camera and spatial information

When you explicitly start a scan, the app uses camera images and supported device depth and motion information. A project may contain source observations, photographs, geometry, dimensions, project names and review notes. These can reveal a private space or people and belongings within it. Scan only where you have permission and avoid capturing information you do not need.

## Room and object photographs

Room source images are retained locally for the optional source-color view. Unsupported color remains unavailable rather than being invented. Room archives currently preserve the measured scan without these source color images.

Object captures are separate from room projects. They retain source photographs locally so you can resume a session or reconstruct a model. Sharing an object model does not include those retained source photographs.

## Storage and deletion

Projects remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Moving a room project to Trash keeps it recoverable; permanent deletion from Trash removes that room's local source data and related exports. Deleting a room does not delete separate object captures.

Deleting an object capture removes that session's local photographs, checkpoint and model. Deletion cannot erase files you previously shared, independently saved copies, or copies in device backups. Manage those copies at their destination and review your device backup settings.

## Import, export and sharing

You choose what to import, export and share using the system document picker and share sheet. Room archives can include spatial observations, geometry and your review notes. Portable room archives remove the original device clock origin while retaining scan event intervals and geometry.

Including source images in a room's 3D export is optional and off by default. If you enable it, the export contains only the images used by the supported color view. Review a bundle before sharing: a spatial model or image can disclose a private place even without your name.

Apps, file providers, recipients and cloud destinations you select handle their copies under their own policies. Device backup services also operate under your device settings. We do not control those services or their retention.

## Permissions and device checks

Camera access is requested for scanning. You can withdraw camera permission in device Settings; new captures then become unavailable. The app checks available storage and device pressure to protect retained work. These operational checks are not sent to us as analytics or included in exported scans.

## If you contact support

Emailing or calling us is optional and separate from scanning. We receive the contact information and message you choose to provide and use them to answer your request and resolve the issue. Our email and communication providers process those communications to deliver the service. Do not send passwords, verification codes or private room captures unless you decide they are necessary and have permission to share them.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve and follow up on your request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us about, correct, or request deletion of information you supplied by contacting Hello@the-3rdact.com. We may need to retain information where law or an unresolved dispute requires it. We cannot access or remotely delete scans stored only on your device.

## This website

These product pages do not add advertising, analytics tags, a scan uploader or a contact form. Visiting the website sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to the hosting service so it can deliver and secure the pages. This is separate from the app's local capture workflow. Opening an email, telephone or company-website link uses the service you choose.
