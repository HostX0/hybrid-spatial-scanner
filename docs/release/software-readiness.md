## Limits the product must explain

Boundary corrections operate only on current explicitly supported graph targets.
Source refinement can invalidate their correspondence. In that case exact
correction intent remains in the journal and is disclosed as an unresolved,
nonmetric review marker, not rebound to a nearby edge. Undo cannot cross an
unproven source/control revision gap. This is not a general CAD solver or an
automatic target-rebasing promise.

Observed object appearance is separate from metric room authority. Unknown faces
and genuine gaps remain unknown. Synthetic fixtures and unit tests do not
establish physical precision, thermal stability, loop-closure behavior, complete
accessibility or superiority to named competing products. No tolerance is widened
to obtain a cleaner-looking plan.

## Distribution and physical gates

After the software gates pass: owner-approved app identity/branding and Apple
team, valid signing/provisioning, installable distribution artifact, required
App Store metadata/privacy labels/support policy and submission review.
Physical phone installation, camera/depth capture, background/locked-device
protection, recovery/relocalization, thermal/storage stress and measurement
acceptance remain explicitly deferred and unverified. A signed binary alone
does not satisfy them. Public competitive claims require lawful retained
challenge data, a fair named/versioned comparison and measured results.

Verdict: meaningful software progress with verified portable repairs, but not yet
an App Store-ready or physically accepted scanner. Finish the bounded remaining
software gates, then signing and actual-device acceptance; do not replace them
with more synthetic test loops.
