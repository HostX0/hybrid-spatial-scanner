## Delivered contract

`EpochRelocalizationPolicy` is a lock-protected, platform-neutral state machine.
It accepts explicitly sequenced run/restart, tracking, interruption, reset,
failure, and stop events. The first continuity-loss signal opens a deterministic
new epoch and disables precision. Repeated limited/unavailable/interruption
callbacks in the same unresolved incident do not churn epoch identities; an
explicit reset or restart always creates a boundary. Only a later normal-tracking
event re-enables precision within the current epoch.

Depth and mesh callbacks share one acquisition-order identity generator. An
epoch switch resets its per-epoch sequence. `ARSessionCoordinator` propagates
each accepted epoch to the single B-004 session driver. Normalized scene-depth
frames and accepted mesh lifecycle results pass the guard before reaching their
precision/metric sinks. A delayed old-epoch observation, a current observation
while precision is unavailable, an unknown epoch, an unstamped mesh result, and
a non-monotonic observation are suppressed and counted. Mesh removals retain
their epoch/sequence/anchor identity in a wrapper; the B-006 lifecycle and its
owned payload semantics are unchanged.

Cross-epoch precise overlay or metric use is denied until an explicit
relationship is accepted. A proposal retains the caller-supplied rigid
column-major transform, proposal identity/sequence, source and target epoch,
and explicit point correspondences. Validation rejects unknown/same epochs,
non-rigid transforms (including reflection/scale), missing/duplicate/nonfinite
or degenerate correspondences, residuals over the configured 0.03 m guard
threshold, and out-of-order proposals. The threshold is a validation policy,
not an accuracy claim. The policy does not estimate a transform, rewrite a
source observation, or produce geometry. Accepted provenance remains visible
in snapshots and `sourceObservationsRemainAuthoritative` remains true.

Reason-specific counters cover epoch transitions, suppression signals,
observation decisions, transform decisions, and cross-epoch use decisions.
The policy snapshot exposes the current epoch, precision gate, open continuity
incident, complete epoch records, accepted relationships, and counters.

No C ABI, JSON schema, mesh lifecycle rule, durable storage, replay codec,
`HSSBridge.swift`, or `hss_engine.cpp` was changed.

## Synthetic and simulator verification

`tests/EpochRelocalizationTests.swift` covers:

- run, normal tracking, relocalizing/limited, unavailable, interruption,
  interruption end, reset, restart, failure, and stop transitions;
- deterministic coalescing of repeated degraded callbacks in one incident;
- stale old-epoch, precision-unavailable, unstamped, unknown, and out-of-order
  observation suppression;
- shared depth/mesh acquisition ordering and sequence reset at an epoch switch;
- valid explicit transform/correspondence acceptance and bidirectional
  relationship authorization without inventing an inverse geometry product;
- reflection/non-rigid, degenerate, high-residual, and out-of-order transform
  rejection; and
- coordinator propagation of epoch boundaries to the owned session driver.

## Integration seam owned by B-013

This change gates the existing precision sinks but intentionally does not
implement the B-013 portable ingress queue or durable B-007/B-008 source
retention. B-013 must route every owned depth/mesh source payload to its
retention/replay path independently of the `EpochObservationDisposition`, and
route only `.accepted` observations to metric ingress. It must also require an
`AcceptedEpochRelationship` before any cross-epoch overlay or metric commit.
An observation marked suppressed means “do not use for current precision,” not
“delete its source.” This seam requires no ABI or schema change in B-012.

## iPhone 15 Pro Max hardware checklist — not run

No physical-device, real relocalization, overlay-registration, LiDAR, or
metrology result is claimed. On an iPhone 15 Pro Max:

1. Record phone model, iOS build, app commit, Xcode/SDK, effective ARKit
   configuration, session identity, orientation, lighting/material conditions,
   and operator. Preserve the epoch event log and all reason counters.
2. Reach normal tracking in epoch A. Capture real scene-depth plus repeated mesh
   revisions around at least three independently identifiable, non-collinear
   physical features. Record each depth/mesh epoch and acquisition sequence.
3. Cause real relocalization using background/foreground and loss/recovery of
   the mapped view; separately exercise `notAvailable`, interruption, an
   explicit tracking reset, and stop/restart. Confirm the first signal of each
   continuity incident hides precise overlays and blocks metric commits, opens
   exactly one epoch, and later degraded callbacks do not churn it. Confirm
   explicit reset/restart always creates a distinct epoch.
4. Deliver deliberately delayed epoch-A depth and mesh callbacks after epoch B
   is current. Confirm their owned source records remain available for audit,
   while precision sinks reject them as stale and increment the exact reason.
5. Before accepting a relationship, revisit the epoch-A area in epoch B. Verify
   no epoch-A precise overlay is drawn in epoch B and no cross-epoch metric
   commit occurs. A frozen old overlay is a failure even if it looks plausible.
6. Build a transform proposal only from retained, explicitly identified real
   correspondences. Verify rigid/non-degenerate/residual validation and retain
   the proposal verbatim. Inject reflection, scale, collinear, high-residual,
   duplicate, and reordered proposals and verify each is rejected without
   moving either source geometry.
7. After explicit acceptance, verify the epoch-A overlay may be related to
   epoch B with the proposal provenance visible. Inspect alignment at all held
   out physical features and report the actual residuals; do not translate the
   0.03 m software guard into a device accuracy claim.
8. Confirm source depth/mesh records and their original epoch-local transforms
   remain unchanged after relationship acceptance, and that rejection/removal
   never fabricates replacement geometry.

The simulator evidence validates deterministic policy, ordering, suppression,
and transform/correspondence checks only. It cannot close the real
relocalization, stale overlay, epoch separation, or physical alignment gate.
