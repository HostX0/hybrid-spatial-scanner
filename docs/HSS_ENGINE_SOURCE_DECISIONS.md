# Hybrid Spatial Scanner — engine source decisions

Research checked 2026-09-22 against primary project repositories, vendor documentation, and papers. The recommendation is a native, offline engine with one Apple capture session, a compact C++ metric/topology core, and custom Metal dense fusion. This is the lowest-risk route to a useful iOS product: no cloud bill, no server dependency, and only one likely third-party runtime library. Open-source projects remain valuable as test oracles and design references, but importing a general 3D/SLAM stack would add more integration and licensing work than product capability.

## Dependency decision

## Native pipeline to implement

1. Normalize each `ARFrame`: validate tracking, depth/confidence buffers, dimensions, strides, scaled intrinsics, pose, epoch, and ownership. Keep calibrated source observations for replay. Invalid or unknown samples remain unknown.
2. Build edge/discontinuity masks and normals without averaging across depth jumps. Select keyframes by motion, new support, tracking and independent viewpoints; repeated stationary frames must not masquerade as independent evidence.
3. Fuse bounded local submaps into an 8³ hashed-block TSDF. Begin experiments at 20 mm structural voxels, an optional 10 mm protected-detail layer, 40 mm truncation, and saturated weights. These are tuning settings, not sensor resolution claims. CPU creates a unique active-block list; Metal owns disjoint voxel writes. Rebuild dirty submaps from retained keyframes after pose corrections.
4. Extract only supported sign changes. Keep display mesh, structural graph and source measurements separate. Fit planes with deterministic robust sampling plus Eigen weighted TLS; retain arbitrary angles, residuals, support extent and view diversity. Build explicit contours/holes and protect narrow returns rather than smoothing them away.
5. Use a small rank-revealing Eigen solve for local hard constraints, with analytic Jacobians, scaling, damping and geometry validity checks. Failed solves leave the accepted snapshot unchanged.

KinectFusion remains the foundational algorithm reference for calibrated depth, TSDF fusion, raycasting and surface tracking, but its Kinect/GPU results do not transfer to an iPhone. [paper](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/kinectfusion-real-time-dense-surface-mapping-tracking/)

## Small-object limits and product claims

Treat room/hall capture as the main measured mode. A specific iPhone 12 Pro study reported roughly centimetre-level accuracy for its tested objects above about 10 cm, declining precision below 10 cm, and an observed detection limit around 5 cm. Those figures came from particular hardware, apps, objects and procedures; they are evidence for caution, not a claim for this engine. [Luetzenburg et al., Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-01763-9)

Attached details around 10–50 cm should trigger a close, multi-angle protected-detail pass and remain provisional until both returns/front faces have independent support. Do not promise metric capture below 10 cm. For sub-5–10 cm, thin, transparent, reflective, repetitive or deformable objects, offer Object Capture as an appearance workflow and request a caliper/laser reference when dimensions matter. Hidden backs and undersides remain unknown.

## Measurement validation before any accuracy claim

Create held-out fixtures for: a surveyed room, a long hall with a closed loop, arbitrary-angle/sloped planes, the 0.50 m × 0.15 m attached-rib adversary, detached columns, and 5–50 cm objects in matte, dark, glossy and transparent materials. Record device model, OS/build, temperature, range, incidence angle, lighting, operator, capture duration and every failure.

Truth must come from a calibrated laser distance meter/total station for rooms and traceable calipers or CMM-grade references for small fixtures, with instrument uncertainty and exact endpoints recorded. Run repeated captures across the oldest and newest supported LiDAR devices and multiple operators. Compare raw depth plane bias, feature recall, signed dimensional error, absolute error percentiles, loop/closure error, area error, completeness, processing time, peak memory and thermal throttling. For cloud/mesh comparisons, permit rigid alignment only—never scale alignment that hides drift.

Gate release per feature class and material using held-out results. Publish median, 95th percentile and worst observed errors plus failure/unknown rates; retain failed scans. Compare ARKit mesh, custom fusion and filtering ablations on the same source records. Until those gates pass, describe measurements as estimates and let users add a reference measurement with provenance and uncertainty.
