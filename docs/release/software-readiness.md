correction intent remains in the journal and is disclosed as an unresolved,
nonmetric review marker, not rebound to a nearby edge. Undo cannot cross an
unproven source/control revision gap. This is not a general CAD solver or an
automatic target-rebasing promise.

and genuine gaps remain unknown. Synthetic fixtures and unit tests do not
establish physical precision, thermal stability, loop-closure behavior, complete
accessibility or superiority to named competing products. No tolerance is widened
