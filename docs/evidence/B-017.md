## Delivered portable proposal module

`hss::surface_proposal::propose_intersection` is a standalone C++20 module
over two already-fitted plane candidates. It does not mutate the boundary
graph, engine, ABI or Swift layer. Each input retains its surface ID, epoch,
weighted support samples, source-observation IDs, correlated diversity groups
and fit diagnostics. An accepted proposal copies both surfaces' fit
diagnostics and reports all-source and direct-intersection provenance
separately.

The module intersects arbitrary unit-normal planes without an axis, gravity,
category or Manhattan prior. It reports the unchanged angle between submitted
normals, the intersection sine and reciprocal conditioning value. The line
direction is canonicalized only for deterministic endpoint ordering; neither
surface normal nor angle is snapped or orthogonalized.

An infinite plane intersection is not itself accepted as a boundary. Support
samples must lie within the caller-declared distance of the conditioned line,
meet caller-declared sample and diversity minima on both surfaces, and supply
overlapping parameter intervals at least as long as the declared minimum.
The returned segment is exactly the overlap of those intervals. A line outside
either observed patch or a gap between disjoint supported intervals is
rejected as extrapolated. The function never closes a loop, invents a corner,
merges surfaces or crosses an unresolved epoch.

All geometric/evidence tolerances are mandatory caller policy. They are not
sensor-accuracy statements. Inputs and diagnostics are validated for finite
values, metric extent, unit normals, UUID identity, support counts, diversity,
weights, plane residuals and resource caps. Failure is atomic: every non-OK
status has an empty proposal.

## Fixtures and focused coverage

The two CSV fixtures under `fixtures/surface_proposals/` are authored
mathematical examples, not captures:

- `acute_supported.csv` retains a supported 59.49-degree normal angle;
- `obtuse_supported.csv` retains a supported 104.25-degree normal angle.

`hss_surface_proposal_test` verifies both non-90-degree results, exact support
clipping, deterministic direction, copied fit diagnostics, per-surface source
provenance and conditioning diagnostics. Programmatic cases reject nearly
parallel planes, nonfinite support, cross-epoch pairs, an infinite line
outside observed patches, disjoint supported intervals, insufficient direct
support, inconsistent fit diagnostics, malformed provenance IDs, duplicate
surface identity, resource-cap violations and undeclared options. Every
rejection asserts that no partial proposal is returned.

The first focused sanitizer run exposed a test-fixture construction mistake:
moving four support samples away from the line collapsed them into duplicate
coordinates, so the intended insufficient-support case correctly failed the
earlier fit-diagnostic consistency gate. The synthetic points were kept
distinct while remaining outside the support band. No implementation
threshold was changed. The clean focused and full runs below include that
repair.

## Scope and tests not run

The module is not wired into robust-fit scheduling, the accepted boundary
graph, `hss_engine`, normalized replay, schemas, the public C ABI, Swift or
Xcode. It proposes only an evidence-bounded line segment; it does not declare
that segment an accepted room edge or infer an interior angle. No
iPhone/simulator build, ARKit/RoomPlan session, real capture, physical
metrology, save/reopen, export, performance, thermal or device timing test ran.

The fixture angles, segment lengths and residuals are synthetic mathematical
checks and make no claim about physical dimensional accuracy or scanner
completion. Later integration must establish candidate adjacency and map an
accepted segment into boundary identities/lineage without discarding source
evidence, crossing epochs, or weakening B-016 topology validation.
