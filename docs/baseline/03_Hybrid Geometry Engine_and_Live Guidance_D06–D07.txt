D06 — Geometry Engine: Reconstruction, Topology & Solvers
1. Engine purpose and invariants
SpatialCore converts calibrated public capture evidence and human intent into a revisioned geometric scene. It is not a wrapper that simply accepts a rectangular RoomPlan result. Preserve arbitrary supported angles, small concavities, protrusions, detached holes and visible ceiling geometry. Never label missing or generatively completed surfaces as observed.
The processing pipeline is validation, spatial indexing, surface proposals, feature graph construction, constraint solving, floor-region/quality evaluation and immutable snapshot publication. Raw evidence and derived fits remain separate. Reject invalid transforms and nonfinite data before they influence topology.
2. Spatial index and evidence
Partition observations into spatial tiles and bounded voxel/sample structures. Initial structure/detail cell sizes are 0.02 m and 0.01 m, respectively; these are processing scales, not sensor resolution or achieved accuracy. Preserve high-curvature transitions and protected features before aggressive downsampling.
Maintain source frame/anchor IDs, pose epoch, ordinal depth confidence and temporal support. Cap contributions from highly correlated stationary frames. A thousand nearly identical samples are not a thousand independent measurements. Anchor updates replace their prior contribution; removals invalidate dependent proposals conservatively.
3. Surface proposals
Use local robust plane fitting, initially RANSAC followed by weighted least squares on supported inliers. A 0.02 m initial inlier threshold is a tuning hypothesis, varied by evidence quality and validated against physical fixtures. Fit arbitrary normals; gravity is a useful orientation cue, not a hard rule that every ceiling is horizontal or every wall vertical.
Evaluate support extent, residuals, normal stability, observation diversity and discontinuities. Do not merge planes solely because their normals are similar. Nearby parallel faces may be a wall, a shelf front or a protrusion. Preserve candidates until attachment and support are sufficient.
Small feature faces need local fitting and edge-aware retention rather than a global minimum-area filter. The 0.15 m front and 0.50 m returns are mandatory adversarial features. A cleaner broad wall plane is not an acceptable substitute.
4. Floor and height model
Estimate a reference floor from supported low-level geometry and gravity alignment; do not select a tabletop because it is the largest horizontal patch. Record the accepted FloorFrame and its evidence. Floor junction, plan cut and ceiling projection are distinct height policies.
A floating shelf must not become a floor obstruction. A sloping wall can intersect the floor differently from the 1.20 m architectural cut. Multiple ceiling heights remain patch-level data. Unobserved floor or ceiling regions retain quality warnings.
5. Boundary graph
Represent boundaries as ordered nodes and edges with stable IDs, surface support and constraints. Fit intersections only when the contributing surfaces are sufficiently supported. Candidate endpoint merging uses an initial 0.03 m tolerance, but protected distinct features cannot be merged merely because they are close.
Outer loops are counterclockwise and holes clockwise in the defined plan basis. Validate closure, self-intersection, adjacency, zero-length edges and containment. A concave room cannot be replaced by its convex hull. An open boundary is not silently closed by a long invented segment; its area remains provisional or unavailable.
Track changes locally. A split creates child edges with provenance to the original; a merge retains references and fails if a protected feature would disappear. Stable identities should survive small coordinate refinements, while topological replacement receives explicit lineage.
6. Protrusions, recesses and obstructions
An attached protrusion is part of the outer boundary chain: approach, return, front, return and continuation. A recess is the corresponding outward indentation relative to room interior. Do not assume symmetry, equal returns or right angles. A detached fixed column is a hole in usable-floor calculations. Attachment uncertainty remains an issue requiring more evidence or user intent.
Movable furniture belongs in a separate layer unless the operator explicitly defines a planning obstacle. Category classification does not create exact geometry. A depth gap alone is not proof of a door, window or passage. Interior wall-face geometry must not imply known structural wall thickness.
7. Constraint model and objective
Solve a local robust objective combining observation residuals, supported surface/edge relationships and explicit user constraints. Engineering weights prioritize evidence and protected topology; they are not calibrated probabilities. A typical objective sums robust weighted residuals for point-to-surface support, edge intersection consistency and declared dimensions, subject to explicit hard constraints.
There is no hidden Manhattan-world prior. Orthogonal, parallel or equal-length relationships are optional constraints only when evidence or explicit user intent justifies them. A typed dimension constrains its declared endpoints; it cannot silently rescale the entire project.
Hard contradictions produce a conflict with relevant constraint IDs and preserve the last valid snapshot. Never weaken an old hard constraint or move unrelated room regions just to make the solve converge. Report a nonminimal conflict set honestly when a minimal-set algorithm is not implemented.
8. Protection versus locking
Protect Feature preserves the existence/order of the feature's topology. Its coordinates can still improve with new evidence. Lock Position freezes specified coordinates and requires a distinct explicit action. A semantic label changes metadata, not geometry. These semantics apply consistently to solver, simplifier, undo and export.
9. Curves, ceilings and missing evidence
Fit curves only where support and residual tests justify them; otherwise use a documented polyline/mesh approximation. Irregular ceilings remain oriented patches and captured mesh when no stable parametric representation exists. Coffers require separate bottom and return faces.
A skylight or open-sky region with missing depth remains unknown. Mirrors and transparent surfaces do not justify a fitted opaque wall. Optional watertight visual completion is an inferred layer, never measured geometry.
10. Incremental execution
Engine mutation is serial. A new frame updates affected tiles, surface candidates and local graph regions. Commands enter the same ordered stream. Publish an immutable accepted snapshot after validation, or a provisional preview clearly separated from durable state. Do not block camera/UI rendering on a long global optimization.
Initial performance targets are p95 incremental update below 30 ms, input acknowledgement below 100 ms and correction preview below 500 ms on a declared baseline workload/device. These are unmeasured targets. Bound queues and defer optional appearance work before compromising measurement validity.
11. Numeric robustness and failure behavior
Use doubles for canonical geometry, stable normalization, finite-value checks and explicit degeneracy handling. Nearly parallel planes, short edges and near-collinear corners require guarded algorithms. Detect rank-deficient solves and return insufficient_evidence rather than huge unstable coordinates.
Every geometry change increments revision and recomputes affected dimensions/quality. An invalid update cannot replace the previous valid snapshot. A blocked region may remain provisional while unrelated valid regions remain reviewable.
12. Required fixtures and acceptance
Synthetic fixtures include a rectangle, six-sided room, acute/obtuse corners, narrow attached protrusion, shallow recess, detached column, multiple holes, nearly collinear edges, sloped ceiling and conflicting constraints. The known protrusion room has area 29.925 m²; adding a 0.30 m × 0.50 m detached column yields 29.775 m² net fixed-obstruction area.
Run the same consented physical difficult-room capture through every major algorithm change. Preserve topology, compare measured errors and inspect missed features. A successful synthetic numerical test is not evidence of physical centimetre accuracy. S02 contains the validation protocol and unexecuted run templates.
D07 — Live Guidance & Human Constraint System
1. Hybrid interaction contract
Automatic capture continues while the user supplies local intent. A correction targets revisioned geometry and is journaled; it never rewrites raw observations. A screen tap alone is not a trustworthy 3D point. Map it through current calibrated camera geometry and valid depth/mesh support. If support is absent, retain a pending ray/region or request another viewpoint.
2. Quality vector
Maintain observed coverage, viewing diversity, depth-valid fraction, fit residual, temporal stability, tracking quality, occlusion suspicion, geometry conflicts and semantic certainty independently. Do not present an internal priority score as “98% accurate.” Measurement uncertainty remains unknown unless calibrated against reference data.
Coverage requires a defined target domain and domain revision. An unobserved ceiling does not have a trustworthy total area for a global completion percentage. A fitted wall behind a wardrobe is not fully observed. Ordinal depth confidence and semantic confidence are not dimensional uncertainty. [SRC-05]
3. Guidance hierarchy and catalog
Validity and safety precede completeness. Show one dominant actionable message, with other issues retained in a tray. Each message has issue_id, code, target_ids, scene_revision, severity, evidence_summary, action, event sequence and expiry condition.
G-001 Tracking limited: stop metric commits and ask for a slow return toward known geometry. G-002 Low light: request illumination. G-003 Low texture: request a view with stable edges/textured surroundings. G-004 Invalid capture distance: request repositioning based on supported range. G-005 Hidden return: identify the feature and request a side view. G-006 Excess motion: ask the user to slow down. G-007 Insufficient baseline: request lateral movement. G-008 Stationary without new evidence: explain why another angle is needed. G-009 Conflicting wall fit: show candidates and offer split/classify/rescan. G-010 Small feature suspected: offer Keep This Detail. G-011 Ceiling incomplete: request safe upward coverage. G-012 Reflective/transparent region: mark uncertainty and offer measured input. G-013 Real processing backlog: show actual work status. G-014 Storage pressure: checkpoint and pause safely. G-015 Constraint contradiction: show conflicts and preserve prior geometry.
Apple already supplies general RoomPlan coaching, including movement and illumination instructions. Our proposed layer adds persistent target association, evidence and reversible constraints. This is not an established market-exclusivity claim. [SRC-06]
4. Timing and message stability
Initial tuning: evaluate quality up to 5 Hz; require 0.8 seconds persistence before a noncritical issue appears; keep it visible at least 2 seconds; suppress repeated haptics for the same issue for 8 seconds. Critical tracking/resource failures bypass debounce. Store all constants in S03 and tune against false/missed alerts.
Dismissing a message suppresses presentation, not the underlying issue. Resolve an issue only when new evidence or an accepted action satisfies its condition. Do not show fake AI timers or ask the operator to wait a fixed minute for a problem that requires movement.
5. Target acquisition
Selection precedence is explicit user target, stable feature hit, valid depth hit, supported plane intersection, then pending ray. A fitted-plane intersection retains fitted provenance. Show a magnified reticle and linked plan inset for narrow faces. Occluded or stale precise overlays must not imply fresh observed support.
When the user marks a corner, protect its topology without automatically locking its coordinates. When a selected feature changed materially since the command's expected revision, require retargeting/confirmation rather than silently applying the edit to a different wall.
6. Command preconditions
mark_corner requires support or explicit pending intent. split_edge requires an existing edge and a split location within its domain. merge_edges requires compatible geometry and no protected intervening feature. mark_protrusion and mark_recess require an ordered support region and parent boundary. mark_obstruction requires a fixed region with attachment status resolved before hole creation.
set_dimension requires value, units, endpoint targets and source. Laser/tape input is user_measured; a guess is inferred. protect_feature prevents topology loss; lock_position freezes coordinates; classify_object changes semantic metadata; mark_unknown prevents false completion; exclude_dynamic_region excludes unreliable moving samples while retaining an auditable event.
Every command returns applied, rejected, conflict or superseded with an actionable reason. Commands and observations are serialized. A command is never acknowledged as saved before the durable journal transaction succeeds.
7. Undo and conflicts
Undo appends an inverse/compensating event rather than erasing history. Redo can become invalid when later topology makes its target ambiguous; explain this instead of applying it to a guessed object. Replaying the journal must reproduce accepted state within tolerance.
Conflicting hard dimensions or locks preserve the prior valid snapshot. Show the relevant constraint set and allow explicit removal/revision. Do not claim the set is mathematically minimal unless that algorithm is implemented and tested.
8. Overlay correctness
Project-space vertices transform through the accepted epoch relationship into the current camera frame and viewport. Test portrait, landscape and rotation during targeting. Hide stale precision during relocalization. Use clipping and appropriate depth testing so a hidden wall is not drawn as if directly observed through furniture.
Observed/fitted/inferred/unobserved outlines differ by line style and text, not color alone. The overlay must not alter measurement geometry for visual convenience.
9. Evaluation and acceptance
Measure task completion, time to correct a feature, unnecessary corrections, false alerts, missed hazards and operator workload. Compare auto-only and hybrid workflows on the same scenes with randomized order. Better animation alone is not evidence of improved geometry.
Required outcomes: a live split survives new observations; undo/redo preserves topology; tracking loss blocks metric commits; a hidden return requests a new view; contradictory dimensions do not corrupt the project; Arabic/English controls are usable; and all core behavior works offline. Actual results remain Not run until evidenced.
