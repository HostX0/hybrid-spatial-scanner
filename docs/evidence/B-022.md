## Delivered standalone guard

`hss::topology_protection::validate_refinement` compares an accepted ordered,
protected feature chain with a candidate update. Both snapshots first pass
bounded standalone validation of canonical IDs, provenance support, explicit
parent lineage, finite bounded coordinates, unique vertices, non-zero
segments, directed adjacency, and non-adjacent segment intersections. Every
segment in this guarded model must explicitly retain topology protection.

The refinement check requires the same feature identity, an increasing
revision, and exactly the same protected vertex/segment count and order. A
candidate entity either retains its corresponding identity or names that exact
predecessor through parent lineage. Removing a segment, reversing the chain,
or substituting an entity without that lineage is rejected. No chain closure,
reordering, simplification, repair, unlock, or generated geometry occurs.

Protected topology and position locking are separate fields. Unlocked,
evidence-retaining coordinates may refine while all protected segments remain
in their order. A position-locked vertex must retain both its lock state and
its exact adopted coordinate. This guard does not implement recorded user
actions for adding/removing protection or locks; an integration must process
such an action transactionally before presenting the resulting accepted
snapshot as a new baseline.

Failures are atomic: every non-OK result has an empty feature optional. The
new module is additive and is not wired into the engine, boundary graph, C
ABI, schemas, Swift/Xcode, solver, persistence, replay, or export.

## Fixture and focused coverage

`fixtures/topology_protection/oblique_protected_chain.csv` is an authored
mathematical fixture, not a sensor capture. It contains an oblique,
unequal-return-style five-segment chain, retained observation IDs, protected
segments, and a separately locked initial vertex.

`hss_topology_protection_test` verifies:

- exact preservation of submitted topology, identities, provenance, and lock
  state;
- accepted evidence-retaining coordinate refinement and a correctly
  lineage-linked vertex/segment replacement;
- atomic rejection of removal, reversal/reordering, and identity substitution
  without predecessor lineage;
- atomic rejection of silent unlock, movement of a position-locked vertex,
  invalid lineage, missing evidence, dropped topology protection, and a
  self-intersecting candidate.

## Scope and limitations

This is a portable C++20 validation guard over caller-supplied snapshots. It
does not establish that source IDs identify independent observations, fit new
coordinates, classify a physical feature, resolve an intentional protection or
lock command, or measure sensor accuracy. The fixture is synthetic and makes
no claim about iPhone capture, feature detectability, physical dimensions,
metrology, performance, thermal behavior, save/reopen, replay, export, or
production scanner completion.
