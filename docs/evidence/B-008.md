## Delivered portable contract

`hss::replay` adds owned C++20 records for epoch relationships, calibrated
normalized scene-depth frames, and mesh-anchor lifecycle events. The
little-endian record format has independent format/schema versions, explicit
type and provenance, a payload length, reserved-field checks, and CRC32; B-007
continues to provide the durable SHA-256 envelope. No C ABI or JSON schema was
changed.

Depth records retain source RGB/depth/confidence layouts, calibrated image crop
and scaled intrinsics, column-major pose, epoch/sequence/monotonic time, copied
Float32 depth, ordinal confidence, and the B-005 validity/fusion/preview masks.
Unavailable confidence remains `255`; low confidence remains valid for preview
but excluded from metric fusion. Mesh records retain UUID, generation,
revision, rigid anchor pose, owned vertices/normals/indices/classifications and
bounds. Removes contain no geometry and identify the exact live
generation/revision they tombstone.

Decoding rejects incompatible format/schema/provenance/type, checksum/length or
reserved-field corruption, nonfinite/nonrigid calibration and poses, malformed
masks/topology, index/count overflow, and per-record/stream resource excess.
Declared array byte requirements are checked before allocation. Replay validates
the complete capture ID, strictly increasing event sequence, monotonic time,
declared epochs, epoch-transform revisions, and add/replace/remove lineage before
calling the explicit typed `EventSink`; a corrupt suffix therefore produces no
callbacks. It does not create geometry or invoke a platform tracker.

## Synthetic and storage coverage

`fixtures/replay/synthetic_trace.expected` is the independent expected trace for
epoch -> depth -> mesh add -> whole replacement -> removal -> UUID re-add. Two
playbacks must emit identical owned events and the same final topology: one live
generation-2 anchor, four vertices, two triangles. Tests also cover corruption,
unsupported versions/types/provenance, allocation/stream bounds, disorder,
missing epoch, revision jumps, sink rejection, and replay of B-007 recovered
depth/mesh payloads in manifest order.

## Limits and next prerequisite

This is portable synthetic/host evidence, not physical sensor parity, metrology,
ARKit replay, or an iOS protected-data/reopen result. iPhone 15 Pro Max capture,
orientation/calibration, interruptions, and save/reopen remain unverified.
Full ENG-03 still requires ENG-02 physical capture evidence and B-010's native
capture-to-plan integration after its B-009/B-011 prerequisites.
